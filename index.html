Guide Questions

1. What is the title of my fairy tale and why did I choose it?
The title of my fairy tale is "The Star of Hope". I choosed this title because the story is about a girl that finds hope in a dark moment of her life. The star represents something bright and beautiful that helps her to find her way. I think this name is good because it resume the main message of the story.
2. Who is my story written for (children or teenagers) and why?
My story is written for teenagers, especially for teenagers between 14 and 17 years old. The reason is that they can understand better the feelings of the principal character and the problems that she faces in the story. I wrote it for teenagers because the lesson at the end is about never giving up and always to believe in your dreams, and I think teenagers needs to hear this message more than childrens.
3. What is my fairy tale about and what message does it communicate?
My fairy tale is about a girl named Elena who lives in a poor village in the mountains. One day, she found a magic star that was fallen from the sky. The star give her the power to see the future and to understand the problems of other people. Elena used this power to help the people in her village. At the end, she realized that the real magic is not the star, but the love and compassion that she have for others. The message is that we can be magical and special if we help others and if we care about them.

Guide Questions (Continued)

4. Which fairy tale elements did I include and why?
I included several fairy tale elements in my story. First, I used a magic object, which is the star. This is a typical element of fairy tales because the magic is very important in this genre. Second, I included a main character (Elena) that is a common person but with a special destiny. Third, I included magical powers that must be used for good things, not for bad things. Fourth, I wrote a conflict in my story when Elena lost the star and she had to find her own power inside her. And finally, I included a happy ending because fairy tales usually have this type of ending where the good person wins and the lesson is learned.
5. What grammatical topics from Selected Topics in English I, did I intentionally use in my story?
I intentionaly use several grammatical topics in my story. I used the Past Simple because the story is narrated in the past, with verbs like "lived", "found", "was". I also used the Present Progressive to describe actions that were happening, for example: "she was walking in the forest". I used the Passive Voice in sentences like "the star was fallen from the sky" and "Elena was chosen by the magic". I included Tag Questions like "she was happy, wasn't she?" and "he could understand, couldn't he?". I used the Passive Voice in future: "the village will be saved by Elena". I also used Modal Verbs like "should", "could" and "must" in sentences like "she should believe in herself" and "she could use her powers". Finally, I used First Conditional like "if she helps the people, she will feel happy" and Second Conditional like "if she didn't have the star, she would find another way".

The Star of Hope

A Fairy Tale

The Star of Hope

Once upon a time, in a small village hidden among the mountains, there lived a girl named Elena. She was about fifteen years old, and her life was not easy. Her family was very poor, and she had to work hard every day to help her parents. Elena lived in a small house at the edge of the village, far away from other people. Every morning, she would go to the forest to collect wood and plants that she could sell in the market.

One dark night, when Elena was walking home from the forest, something magical happened. A bright light fell from the sky like a comet. Elena ran towards the light, and when she arrived at the place, she found a beautiful star on the ground. The star was glowing with a soft, golden light. It was the most beautiful thing that Elena had ever seen in her entire life. She took the star carefully in her hands, and immediately, she felt a warm sensation running through her body.

Elena finds the magic star in the forest.

That night, Elena could not sleep because she was thinking about the star. She looked at it for hours, and as she observed it, she began to understand something strange. The star was giving her visions of the future. She could see what would happen to the people in her village. She could see their problems, their sadness, and their hopes. It was as if the star had chosen her for a special purpose.

The next morning, Elena decided that she would use the star's power to help her village. She went to see a woman named Maria, whose son was sick. Elena touched the star, and suddenly, she could see exactly what Maria's son needed. She gave Maria advice about special herbs that would help her son recover. Maria was surprised because Elena knew so much about medicine, but Elena did not tell her about the star.

Day after day, Elena used the star's magic to help more and more people. If a farmer needed to know when to plant his crops, Elena would look at the star and tell him. If someone was lost in the mountains, Elena would see their location through the star's visions. If a family did not have money for food, Elena would find a way to help them. The village began to change. People started to trust each other more. The crops grew better. The sick people recovered. Happiness was returning to the village.

Elena helps the villagers with the star's magic.

However, Elena did not realize that someone had been watching her. There was a jealous man named Victor who lived in the village. He wanted to be important and famous, but nobody respected him because he was selfish and unkind. Victor saw that Elena was becoming more and more respected by the villagers, and he became very angry. One night, while Elena was sleeping, Victor broke into her house and stole the star.

Victor steals the star while Elena sleeps.

When Elena woke up and found that the star was gone, she felt devastated. For the first time since she found the star, she cried. She thought that without the magic star, she could not help anyone anymore. She thought that her purpose was lost. Elena felt so sad that she wanted to give up.

Elena cries when she discovers the star is gone.

But then, something incredible happened. As Elena was crying, she heard a voice in her heart. The voice told her that the real magic was not in the star, but inside her own soul. The star had only shown her what she already had inside: the ability to care, to understand, and to help others. Elena realized that she did not need the star to be special. She was already special because of who she was.

With this understanding, Elena stood up and went to find Victor. When she found him, instead of being angry, she approached him with compassion. She told Victor that she understood why he was sad and why he wanted the star. She told him that he could also be important and respected if he learned to care about others. Victor was so surprised by Elena's kindness that he felt ashamed of what he had done. He gave the star back to Elena and apologized sincerely.

Elena talks to Victor kindly. He gives back the star.

Elena took the star and walked to the top of the mountain. There, under the light of the moon, she released the star back into the sky. As it rose higher and higher, it became brighter and brighter. Elena watched it disappear among the other stars, and she smiled. She realized that the star did not belong to her. It had been sent to teach her a lesson.

Elena releases the star back to the sky.

From that moment on, Elena continued to help the people in her village, but now without the star's magic. She used her intelligence, her kindness, and her compassion. She became a teacher, and she taught young people how to read and write. She helped farmers improve their techniques. She became a healer, and she used her knowledge of herbs to cure sick people. The village prospered, and Elena became known throughout the region as a wise and good woman.

Years later, when Elena was an old woman, young people would ask her about the stories of the magical star. Elena would smile and say that the real magic was always within us. She would tell them that if we believe in ourselves and care about others, we can create our own miracles. We do not need magic stars from the sky; we have magic inside our hearts.

And so, Elena lived a happy and meaningful life, knowing that the most powerful magic in the world is the love and compassion that we share with others. The village she helped became a model for other villages, and the lesson that Elena learned that night has been passed down through generations.

Old Elena teaches young people the lesson of the star.

Justificación

La Importancia de la Lectura y la Escritura en Segundo Idioma

La lectura es una de las herramientas más importantes para el desarrollo personal, intelectual y cultural de niños y jóvenes. A través de la lectura, las personas pueden acceder a nuevas ideas, culturas y perspectivas del mundo. Los libros y los cuentos nos permiten viajar a lugares lejanos sin tener que mover de nuestro asiento, y nos ayudan a desarrollar la imaginación y la creatividad. Además, la lectura mejora la concentración, el vocabulario y las habilidades de pensamiento crítico. Por lo tanto, es fundamental promover el hábito de leer desde edades tempranas.

Por otro lado, aprender a escribir y leer en un segundo idioma como el inglés es cada vez más relevante en nuestro mundo globalizado. El inglés es la lengua internacional de la ciencia, la tecnología, los negocios y la cultura. Si una persona puede escribir y hablar en inglés, tendrá acceso a oportunidades académicas y laborales que de otro modo no tendría. Además, leer y escribir en inglés nos permite comunicarnos con millones de personas alrededor del mundo y entender la información que está disponible en este idioma.

El inglés puede influir positivamente en el desarrollo académico, cultural y laboral de los jóvenes. Académicamente, muchas universidades importantes en el mundo exigen el dominio del inglés, y muchos libros y recursos educativos están disponibles solamente en inglés. Culturalmente, a través del inglés podemos acceder a películas, música, literatura y arte de diferentes partes del mundo. Laboralmente, la mayoría de las empresas internacionales buscan empleados que hablen inglés, y esto abre muchas puertas para el futuro profesional de una persona.

Un aspecto muy importante que he aprendido en este proyecto es que es crucial crear textos originales y no copiarlos. Cuando copiamos textos de otros, no estamos aprendiendo realmente. El acto de crear nuestros propios textos nos enseña a pensar, a estructurar nuestras ideas y a expresarlas de manera clara. Además, copiar es deshonesto y afecta nuestro aprendizaje académico. Este proyecto me permitió entender que el proceso de crear un cuento en inglés, aunque fue difícil, me ayudó a aprender más sobre la gramática y el vocabulario que sobre simplemente copiar algo de internet.

En conclusión, creo que este proyecto fue muy valioso para mi aprendizaje. Me dio la oportunidad de practicar el inglés de una manera creativa, de aplicar la gramática que he aprendido en clase, y de entender la importancia de la honestidad académica y la originalidad. Seguiré leyendo en inglés y escribiendo mis propias historias para mejorar mis habilidades en este idioma importante.
